AI "Why Did This Happen?" Analyzer

An enterprise-grade diagnostic analytics platform combining statistical hypothesis testing, machine learning pipelines, SHAP & LIME explainability, and multi-evidence root cause rankers to determine why unexpected outcomes occur.

1. Multi-Evidence Scoring

Statistical delta, ML feature importance, SHAP marginal shifts, and LIME local coefficients synthesized into unified attribution.

2. Counterfactual Simulator

Interactive "What-If" sensitivity testing and segment contribution breakdown.

3. Automated Reports

One-click generation of audit-ready PDF, Word (DOCX), HTML, and JSON root cause dossiers.
